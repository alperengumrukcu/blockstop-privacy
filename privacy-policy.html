Blockstop Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what information is processed when you play Blockstop (Android and iOS), why, and what rights you have. The game does not require an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address or phone number.

1. Controller and contact

Alperen Gümrükçü (individual developer).
Contact: alperen.gumrukcu@gmail.com

2. What information is processed

2.1 Information stored only on your device

Your progress (unlocked stops, stars), coins and power-ups, settings (sound, vibration, notifications, language), daily reward and streak data, and whether you bought the ad-free version are stored only on your phone. They are not sent to us or to any server. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

2.2 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob to show ads. When an ad is shown, Google may collect and process the following from your device:

  • IP address (which may be used to estimate the general location of your device),
  • advertising identifiers (Android advertising ID; on iOS the IDFA if you allow it) and app set ID,
  • interactions such as app launches, taps and video views,
  • diagnostic information such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage.

Purposes: serving ads, measuring ad performance, analytics and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. For details see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

2.3 Consent and your choices

  • In the EU, UK and similar regions, Google's consent form (UMP) is shown when you first open the app. You decide whether to allow personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options (the row is shown only where required).
  • On iOS, the system asks whether you allow tracking for advertising. If you decline, ads are not personalized and the game works the same. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads.

2.4 Purchases

Coin packs and the ad-free version are bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Only those stores process your card and payment details; they never reach us. The app only receives confirmation from the store that a purchase happened and records the item on your device.

2.5 Notifications

Reminder notifications (for example the daily reward) are scheduled locally on your phone and are not sent through a server. You can turn them off in the app settings or your phone settings at any time.

3. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone other than the parties above.

4. Legal basis

In the EU/UK (GDPR) we rely on your consent (UMP form) for advertising identifiers and personalization, on performance of a contract for what is needed to run the game, and on legitimate interests for debugging and security. In Türkiye, the data is processed in line with Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

5. Retention

Information on your device stays until you uninstall the app. Retention of information collected by Google is governed by Google's policies.

6. International transfers

Google may process data in countries outside Türkiye and the EU (for example the United States), under safeguards such as Google's standard contractual clauses.

7. Your rights

Under KVKK Article 11 and the GDPR you have the right to know whether your data is processed, to request information, to correct or delete it, to object to processing and to withdraw consent. Because the game does not identify you, most data exists only on your device; uninstalling the app deletes it. For other requests, write to the email address above. You may also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or your local data protection authority in the EU.

8. Children

Blockstop is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If we learn that a user is under 13, we will delete the related data.

9. Security

Data is kept on your device inside the app's protected storage. Communication with Google is encrypted in transit.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. For significant changes we will notify you in the app or on the store page. The current version is always on this page.
